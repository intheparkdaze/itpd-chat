Credits

Add, rename, set tiers, take people off the credits, or delete them. Hit Save and the stream updates within about 15 seconds.
In YouTube Studio go to Earn > Memberships > Your members, select the whole page (Ctrl+A), copy it, and paste it into the box below (here in the editor, not into the file). The changes appear right away; then tap Apply changes, then Save. Only YouTube tiers change: mods, Twitch and Prime stay as they are.
